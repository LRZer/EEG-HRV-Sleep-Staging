Source: https://sleeptight.isr.uc.pt/?page_id=48
Citation: Khalighi S, Sousa T, Santos JM, Nunes U. ISRUC-Sleep: A comprehensive public
dataset for sleep researchers. Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine 124:180-192 (2016).
The provider makes the dataset publicly available for research and requests citation.
No separate permissive redistribution license is stated on the source page.
Dataset rights and provider conditions remain with the original authors; this project's
software license does not relicense datasets. Raw ISRUC files are acquired from the original
provider, rather than redistributed here; the archive provides the prepared research inputs.

Derived experimental inputs use single-channel EEG, ECG-derived time-domain HRV,
five-class labels, per-epoch manifests, training/test rosters and past-only context indices.
They are transformations of the named public research datasets, not newly collected subjects.
